| T10 | Reporting and dashboard module | This will be the part of the software that generates reports. The dashboard pulls the figures from the other modules for a date range the user chooses and presents them to management as reports on customer service, loyalty, and partner activity. | T11 | Integration and system testing | Bring the modules together, test the end-to-end scenarios, check access control against each role, and fix what the testing finds. | T10 | 9 |
