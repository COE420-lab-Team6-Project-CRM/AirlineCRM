Team Consolidated Requirements

FR-01 "Create/Update Customer Profile" "The system shall allow a Customer Service Representative (CSR) to create and update a customer profile, capturing full name, contact details (email, phone number, mailing address), date of birth, and preferred language." [Contributor: Ahmed Sherif]
FR-02 "Locate Customer Profile" "The system shall allow a CSR to locate a customer profile by searching on any one of the following identifiers: full name, customer ID, email address, phone number, or booking reference, and shall display all matching profiles when more than one match is found." [Contributor: Ahmed Sherif]
FR-03 "Display Booking & Travel History" "The system shall display, for a selected customer, a read-only list of their reservations, flights, and cancellations, sourced from imported reservation data and ordered by travel date (most recent first)." [Contributor: Ahmed Sherif]
FR-04 "Log Service Request" "The system shall allow a CSR to log a new inquiry, request, or complaint against a specific customer profile, recording a category (e.g. Billing, Baggage, Flight Delay, Service Quality), a priority level (Low/Medium/High/Urgent), a free-text description, and an assignment to a staff member for handling." [Contributor: Ahmed Sherif]
FR-05 "Progress Service Request Workflow" "The system shall allow assigned staff to progress a logged service request through a defined status workflow (Open -> In Progress -> Resolved -> Closed), require a resolution note before a request can be marked Closed, and automatically record a time-stamped audit trail entry for every status or assignment change." [Contributor: Ahmed Sherif]
FR-06 "Record Customer Feedback" "The system shall allow a CSR to record customer feedback against a specific booking or service interaction. Each entry shall include a satisfaction rating on a defined scale and a feedback category. A free-text comment will be optional." [Contributor: Chihab Ben Khadra]
FR-07 "Calculate Satisfaction Trends" "The system must calculate satisfaction trends over a selectable time period and display average ratings by category. Categories whose average rating falls below a configurable threshold of around 3.00/5.00 shall be flagged for review." [Contributor: Chihab Ben Khadra]
FR-08 "Display Loyalty Status" "The system shall display a customer's current loyalty point balance and membership tier. It shall also display the full tier history, listing the date and reason for each tier change." [Contributor: Chihab Ben Khadra]
FR-09 "Adjust Loyalty Points" "The system will allow an authorized staff member to manually adjust a customer's loyalty point balance. The adjustment shall require a reason code and a linked note, and the system shall reject the adjustment if either is missing." [Contributor: Chihab Ben Khadra]
FR-10 "Manage Corporate/Partner Account" "The system shall allow an account manager to create and maintain a corporate or partner account record. The record shall capture the organization name, agreement dates, negotiated terms, and the assigned account manager, and shall support logging interaction entries against the account." [Contributor: Chihab Ben Khadra]
FR-11 "Define Customer Segment" "The system shall allow users to define and save a customer segment which may include loyalty tier, route, flights within a duration, and complaint history, and shall depict the match count before saving." [Contributor: Rizwanul Abidin Asim]
FR-12 "Create Marketing Campaign" "The system shall allow users to create a marketing campaign targeting a saved segment, generating one personalised communication per member which is stored on the customer profile." [Contributor: Rizwanul Abidin Asim]
FR-13 "Manage Staff Accounts and Roles" "The system shall allow authorized users to create, modify, and deactivate staff accounts, reject actions for which a role is disallowed, and log denied attempts." [Contributor: Rizwanul Abidin Asim]
FR-14 "Create Notification Record" "The system must create a notification record for booking updates, flight changes, and service responses, tracking notification status as pending, sent, or failed." [Contributor: Rizwanul Abidin Asim]
FR-15 "Operational Dashboard" "The system should provide a dashboard for a chosen date range displaying request volume, resolution time, complaint categories, loyalty distribution, and marketing campaign results, all exportable as a PDF or CSV file on demand." [Contributor: Rizwanul Abidin Asim]
