Team Consolidated Scenarios
S-01 "Locating and Updating a Returning Customer's Profile" "A returning customer, Omar Al-Farsi, calls the airline's support center after changing his phone number and moving to a new address. The CSR does not recognize him immediately, so she searches for his profile using his email address. The search returns his existing profile, and the CSR verifies his identity using his date of birth and a recent booking reference, then updates his phone number, mailing address, and preferred language. Had no matching profile been found, the CSR would instead have created a new customer profile capturing the same details."
S-02 "CSR Logs and Escalates a Repeat Customer Complaint" "A customer, Sara Haddad, emails the airline to complain that her checked baggage was damaged and that a previous refund request was never resolved. A CSR searches for Sara's profile by email, opens her consolidated view, and sees two other unresolved requests logged in the past month. The CSR logs a new complaint against her profile, categorizing it as Baggage with High priority given the repeat nature of the issue, and assigns it to a senior staff member. As the complaint moves from Open to In Progress to Resolved, the CSR adds resolution notes explaining the compensation offered, and the system automatically records each status change in an audit trail."
S-03 "Post-Flight Feedback" "A customer, Layla Kheder, submits a feedback form after a delayed flight, rating her overall experience two out of five and describing the delay and a missing meal service. The CSR opens Layla's consolidated view and reviews her feedback history, noticing this is her second low rating in three months. Because Layla is a Silver tier member and the airline's service recovery policy applies, the CSR manually adds 2,000 goodwill loyalty points to her account, entering a reason code and a short note. The adjustment appears immediately in Layla's points balance and is recorded in the loyalty audit trail together with the CSR's staff ID and the linked feedback record."
S-04 "Reviewing a Corporate Account Ahead of a Renewal Meeting" "An account manager, Ali Eissa, prepares for a renewal meeting with a corporate client, MyExample Consulting Group, whose staff travel under a negotiated fare agreement. He reviews the agreement's end date and its negotiated discount terms. He also checks the volume commitments agreed the previous year. He then opens the account's interaction log, which lists the recent engagement touchpoints recorded by the sales team, and adds a new entry summarizing a call in which MECG requested an increase to their annual booking allowance. Ali flags the account for follow up once the finance team confirms updated pricing."
S-05 "Targeted Promotion for Frequent Dubai-London Travellers" "Khaled Al Khazraji defines a segment for Gold and Platinum Customers who flew Dubai to London at least 3 times in the past 12 months with no unresolved complaint. The system returns 5,137 matches. He saves it as 'Gold DXB-LHR Frequent Flyers' and creates a campaign offering 35% bonus points on bookings before 15th November, 2026. One personalised message is generated by the system per customer and recorded on their profile. Two weeks later the campaign summary shows 1,359 targeted customers booked on the route via the campaign."
