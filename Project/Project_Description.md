The Airline Customer Relationship Management (CRM) System is a centralized software solution designed to help an airline company efficiently manage and improve its interactions with customers, business clients, and partner organizations throughout the entire travel and business relationship lifecycle. The system will integrate customer information, flight bookings, inquiries, complaints, feedback, loyalty programs, and communication history into a single platform.

Beyond individual travelers, airlines also maintain ongoing business relationships with corporate/enterprise clients who book travel at scale, as well as with external partner businesses the airline depends on to operate, such as in-flight catering providers and aircraft maintenance companies. The CRM will also serve as a hub for these B2B relationships, allowing staff to manage enterprise client accounts and agreements, and to track and coordinate interactions with external vendor and service partners, alongside standard customer relationship management.

The CRM will enable airline staff to maintain detailed customer and business client profiles, track reservations and travel history, manage customer service requests, and respond to complaints or inquiries efficiently. It can also support customer segmentation and personalized communication, allowing the airline to provide targeted offers, promotions, and travel updates.

The system will include different user roles, such as CRM administrators, customer service representatives, marketing staff, account managers handling corporate/enterprise clients, and managers, with access based on their responsibilities. Management dashboards and reports will provide insights into customer satisfaction, booking patterns, complaints, loyalty activity, partner engagement, and overall customer relationship health.

The main goal of the project is to improve customer satisfaction, streamline airline customer service operations, strengthen customer loyalty, support the airline's B2B and partner relationships, and provide management with useful data for informed decision making.

### Key Features
Customer Management: Create and maintain customer profiles and contact information.
Booking & Travel History: Track reservations, flights, cancellations, and customer travel history.
Customer Service Management: Record and manage inquiries, requests, and complaints.
Feedback & Satisfaction: Collect customer feedback and monitor satisfaction levels.
Loyalty Program Management: Manage loyalty points, membership levels, and rewards.
B2B & Partner Management: Manage corporate/enterprise client accounts and track relationships with external partner businesses (e.g. catering, maintenance).
Marketing & Communication: Send personalized promotions, notifications, and travel related communications.
Employee/User Management: Manage system users and role based access.
Reports & Dashboard: Provide management with customer, partner, and operational analytics.
Notification Management: Handle booking updates, flight notifications, and customer service responses.
