The Airline Customer Relationship Management (CRM) System is a centralized software solution designed to help an airline company efficiently manage and improve its interactions with customers throughout the entire travel journey. The system will integrate customer information, flight bookings, inquiries, complaints, feedback, loyalty programs, and communication history into a single platform.

The CRM will enable airline staff to maintain detailed customer profiles, track reservations and travel history, manage customer service requests, and respond to complaints or inquiries efficiently. It can also support customer segmentation and personalized communication, allowing the airline to provide targeted offers, promotions, and travel updates

The system will include different user roles, such as CRM administrators, customer service representatives, marketing staff, and managers, with access based on their responsibilities. Management dashboards and reports will provide insights into customer satisfaction, booking patterns, complaints, loyalty activity, and overall customer engagement.

The main goal of the project is to improve customer satisfaction, streamline airline customer service operations, strengthen customer loyalty, and provide management with useful data for informed decision making.

### Key Features
Customer Management: Create and maintain customer profiles and contact information.
Booking & Travel History: Track reservations, flights, cancellations, and customer travel history.
Customer Service Management: Record and manage inquiries, requests, and complaints.
Feedback & Satisfaction: Collect customer feedback and monitor satisfaction levels.
Loyalty Program Management: Manage loyalty points, membership levels, and rewards.
Marketing & Communication: Send personalized promotions, notifications, and travel related communications.
Employee/User Management: Manage system users and role based access.
Reports & Dashboard: Provide management with customer and operational analytics.
Notification Management: Handle booking updates, flight notifications, and customer service responses.
