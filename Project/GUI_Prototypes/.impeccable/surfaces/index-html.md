# Surface brief: AirlineCRM CSR prototype (index.html)

Scope: four Operate screens for the Customer Service Representative (home, UC-01 Locate Customer, UC-04 Booking & Travel History, UC-09 Adjust Loyalty Points), static clickable prototype, desktop-first, screenshotted for report Section 9. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: a CSR on a call identifies the caller, reads their travel, adjusts points under the 3,000-point review rule. Must look like a shipping product and stay readable in print. Avoid generic templates and flashy effects.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The CRM is set like an airline timetable and route map: every customer, booking and point is a row in one exact grid, and travel is drawn as arcs from the hub. It refuses the boxed-card SaaS admin (white cards, blue buttons, KPI tiles).
OWN-WORLD: Spruce livery field (#0b4f43) owns the left rail; cool paper ground (#f4f5f2); ink #121614; amber (#d99a0e) only on routes, today and the live selection. Archivo throughout, condensed widths for codes and column heads, tabular figures. No card borders or shadows; regions are colour fields, rules and type scale.
STORY: The CSR sees what needs a call, finds the caller by any identifier, opens one profile, reads the route map and exact-scale timeline, and adjusts points against a visible 3,000-point scale.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: spruce rail left; a large date line and one-sentence finding top-left with "Find customer" right; a four-figure timetable strip; then notifications and requests as timetable rows, request age drawn as proportional bars.
FORM: Timetable & Route Map, position 3 of my ordered list, seed key 14a33c70. Raises: exact proportional time (labanotation); chrome deleted (metro); tight tabular figures (datamatics); focused result expands in place (streaming); tabs show their extent (acetate). Signature interaction: the selected search row expands in place; hovering a booking lights its arc on the route map; the points scale moves as the CSR types.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
