This feasibility study discusses the practical viability of developing the proposed Airline Customer Relationship Management System within an academic timeframe. Building on our team's Computer Science and Computer Engineering background and prior CRM internship experience, we believe our project is therefore feasible.

### 1. Technical Feasibility

* Skill Alignment: As CS and CE students with prior internship experience in CRM development, the team possesses direct expertise in database management and web service design.
* Tech Stack: Development will utilize standard open-source tools, including modern web frameworks, relational databases, backend server environments, and mock APIs for external flight system integration.
* Hardware Infrastructure: Development and testing will take place entirely on personal student laptops without requiring custom hardware components.

### 2. Economic Feasibility

* Development Costs: Financial expenses are minimal because the team relies on open-source software and free cloud hosting services.
* Required Resources: For the time being, primary resources consist of personal devices, IDEs, Git and Github as version control platforms, database engines, and free tier core stack of Node.js and React.js accounts, as well as free accounts for frontend and backend deployment. We can use free tools such as Vercel for frontend, Render for backend, pgAdmin and Neon for database.
* Resource Limitations: Storage and bandwidth caps on free hosting platforms limit high-volume stress testing, for instance, Render is limited by 100 GB/month bandwidth, and Neon possesses 0.5GB storage. we will assume such margins will be acceptable for our project. 

### 3. Operational Feasibility

* Problem Resolution: Nowadays a reasonable number of industries are moving to centralize customer records, travel histories, service inquiries, loyalty rewards, and communications on a single platform. Many airlines are yet to make this shift yet, which means our service is demanded and will be of use to the stakeholder and a high likelyhood of user acceptance .
* Decision Support: Operational dashboards provide actionable customer analytics to support data driven decision making for airline leadership.

### 4. Schedule Feasibility

* Deliverable Execution: Core deliverables, such as customer profile creation and complaint logging, can easily be completed within the course duration of roughly 8 weeks.
* Scope Management: Advanced capabilities like automated notifications and deep analytics dashboards will be built as modular extensions to prevent schedule delays.

### 5. Overall Feasibility
The Airline CRM project is fully viable across technical, economic, operational, and schedule parameters. The primary project constraint is the limited course duration, which the team will manage by delivering core profile and booking modules before integrating optional analytics features.
