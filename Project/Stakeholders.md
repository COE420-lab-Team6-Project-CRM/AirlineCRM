# Stakeholder Analysis

The following table identifies the main stakeholders of the Airline CRM system, their role in
the project, what they expect from it, the influence they have over it, and how the system
affects them.

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest / Expectations | Influence on the System | How the System Affects the Stakeholder |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Customer service representatives | Main daily users. They handle inquiries and complaints. | Require quick customer lookup and full interaction history in one place. | High. Their way of working defines the service request workflow, as well as major screen layout. | Several separate systems can be replaced with one profile, but they must log every interaction for security. |
| Account managers | They manage corporate client accounts and partner businesses. | Expect a proper record of agreements and contact with catering, maintenance partners, etc. | High for the B2B module, since they define what an account record must hold. | Informal tracking replaced with structured records they are responsible for keeping updated, making the whole process seamless and easy to manage. |
| Airline management | Sponsor the system monetarily. Also use the dashboards and reports. | Need clear insight into satisfaction, complaints, loyalty activity and partner engagement. | High as their reporting needs would shape the dashboard, and they decide whether the system is adopted and implemented. | System gives them consolidated data for decisions instead of mere figures or unstructured data from separate systems. |
| Customers (passengers and corporate clients) | External parties whose data is held in the system. They are recorded but are not users of it. | Faster and more consistent service, accurate records, and privacy of their data. | Low directly, but their complaints and feedback decide what the system must record. | Receive better service, while their personal data is stored in one place and must be protected, hence secure retrieval of data is needed. |
| Development team (Team 6) | Analyse, design, build and test the system. | A scope that fits the semester and an even share of the work for the team of 3 students. | Very high. Every design, technology and scheduling decision is taken by the team. | Develop the entire CRM. The agreed scope is what keeps it manageable and feasible. |
| Marketing staff | Build customer segments and run ad campaigns. | Useful segmentation criteria and visibility of campaign results. | Medium. They define the segmentation criteria and what the campaign tools must do. | Systems allows targeted communication in addition to sending broadcast messages to everyone. |
| CRM administrator | Manage user accounts, roles and permissions. | Clear role definitions and a record of sensitive actions. | Medium. Defines the access control model separating the staff roles. | Gains central control of access, and becomes responsible for assigning permissions correctly. |
